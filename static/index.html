Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful insights into video extraction, quality settings, and platform compatibility.

How does OmniStream provide 1080p, 4K, and 8K with sound? ▼
Platforms like YouTube store high-resolution video streams (1080p, 1440p, 4K, 8K) separately from the audio stream to conserve bandwidth. OmniStream uses an integrated high-performance FFmpeg engine to seamlessly merge the highest quality video and audio tracks together into a single standard MP4 file on the fly!
Can I extract only the background music or song? ▼
Yes! Click the Download Audio Only (Music) tab after entering any link. You can choose from Studio 320 kbps MP3, 256 kbps, 192 kbps, 128 kbps, or original M4A/AAC without wasting data downloading the video stream.
What about Disney+ Hotstar, Netflix, and Prime Video? ▼
Commercial streaming subscription services like Disney+ Hotstar, Netflix, and Prime Video protect their commercial movies and series using Widevine DRM (Digital Rights Management) encryption. Third-party extractors cannot decrypt DRM-protected streams legally and technically. For those services, use their official apps' offline download feature.
How do I download WhatsApp videos or status? ▼
WhatsApp messages and status updates are end-to-end encrypted and do not have public web URLs. WhatsApp status and received media are already saved directly into your device's internal storage (e.g. WhatsApp/Media/Statuses).
How to download links from Google Chrome? ▼
Google Chrome is a web browser. Whenever you find a video, reel, or track in Chrome, click the address bar, copy the URL (or right-click the video and choose "Copy link address"), then paste it right here into OmniStream!
